Who we are

India's national professional body for Strength & Conditioning

The Indian National Strength & Conditioning Association (INSCA) is a national professional organization established to advance the science, education, and application of Strength & Conditioning across India.

INSCA serves as a platform for coaches, physiotherapists, sports scientists, fitness professionals, researchers, and educators — providing internationally aligned education, certification programs, workshops, conferences, and collaborative opportunities. Our goal is to raise professional standards, encourage research and innovation, and contribute to healthier individuals, stronger athletes, and a more physically prepared nation.

Our Vision

A global leader in applied sports science

To establish India as a global leader in Strength & Conditioning education, research, and applied sports science — developing world-class professionals and creating internationally recognized standards of excellence.

INSCA, defined

The primary objective of INSCA is to advance the principles, education, training, research, and practical application of Strength & Conditioning in India. By integrating scientific evidence with practical application, INSCA strengthens India's sports performance ecosystem while contributing to the global advancement of the field.

Founder's message

A vision rooted in India's heritage

President — INSCA
Dr. Arugan C. ArjunFounder & President — INSCA
“
It has always been our dream and mission to restore India's historic leadership in the fields of health, physical excellence, and human performance.

India has contributed immensely to the world through Ayurveda, Siddha, Yoga, Martial Arts, and holistic well-being. These timeless systems have shaped global understanding of health and performance for centuries. One important dimension that deserves renewed focus is Strength and Physical Conditioning.

INSCA was established to bridge this gap by combining India's rich heritage with modern sports science, international collaboration, and evidence-based practice. Through education, certification, research, and innovation, we aspire to develop professionals who will contribute to stronger athletes, healthier communities, and a more resilient nation.

This initiative is not merely an organization — it is a long-term commitment to building a stronger future for India and inspiring excellence across the world.

Dr. Arugan C. Arjun
President, INSCA

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is INSCA?
The Indian National Strength & Conditioning Association (INSCA) is a national professional organization established to advance the science, education, certification and applied practice of Strength & Conditioning across India. Registration No. DRB3/SOR/103/2024–2025.
What certification does the Level 1A workshop provide?
You receive a lifetime INSCA Level 1A Certification in Strength & Conditioning along with a CPR certification. The program is accredited by INSCA and supported by the Sports Development Authority of Tamil Nadu (SDAT).
Who should attend?
S&C coaches, fitness coaches, personal trainers, physiotherapists, sports physiotherapists, sports scientists, biomechanists, athletic trainers, rehabilitation specialists, physical education teachers, sports students, academy owners and sports enthusiasts.
How do I register?
Register instantly via WhatsApp at +91 8277713616, or call the same number. Seats are limited, so early registration is recommended.
Where and when is the workshop held?
